This page is a stub. It covers the basics and will grow.

Intelligence agencies, operatives, and operations come from the La Résistance expansion and work as they do in the base game. Millennium Dawn adds its own agency upgrades and operations. Agency upgrades are selected manually in the Intelligence Agency window.
