Howdy fellow strategy enthusiasts!
Antarctica is the only continent (almost) untouched by human presence. World leaders agreed to focus entirely on research without disrupting the wildlife and natural landscape. Since Hearts of Iron is a military strategy game, what is there to do on a piece of land where resource mining and military operations are absolutely prohibited?

I don't know, enjoy Antarctica! Bye!

Oh wait, there is one thing actually, research! (and a bit of politics)

## Part I - Antarctic Treaty
The Treaty is a way to connect countries interested in Antarctica. Irl it's a set of rules and guidances how the countries should develop their presence on this continent. That includes earlier mentioned prohibitions and environmental regulations. Anyone can join the treaty.

Treaty members gain access to 2 more smaller treaties, CCAS and CCAMLR. Both of them were established to protect the wildlife of Antarctica. In Millennium Dawn, signing those treaties will grant increased output of several outputs developed by the Research Stations.
