Special projects are long research efforts carried out at research facilities. Millennium Dawn has four facility buildings: Land, Air, Naval, and Nuclear.

## What Millennium Dawn Offers

Projects are grouped by field: air, artillery, infantry equipment, land, tank modules, missiles, naval, nuclear, radar, rockets, and civilian. A few countries have projects of their own, and the Antarctica and Event Horizon content adds more.

## Game Rules

Two game rules change which projects exist. Both are under Miscellaneous Rules.

- **Futuristic Special Project**: Allowed (default) or Disabled. Disabled removes the futuristic projects.
- **Nuclear Weapons Research**: Allowed (default), Players Only, or Disabled. Players Only stops the AI from starting the nuclear weapon projects. Disabled hides them for everyone.
