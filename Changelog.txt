v2.1.0

AI:
 - An AI in a peacetime deficit now trims a civil spending law each month and lowers what its people expect with it, instead of waiting for a deep crisis, and restores spending once its surplus returns

Balance:
 - Reduced the starting inflation to 12.6% and increased the starting policy rate of Iran to 8

Bugfix:
 - Corrected copy-pasted event option log ids (Issue #5266)
 - Fixed zombies never spawning when the Zombie Spawn Delay rule is set to no delay (Issue #5295)
 - Auto-upgrade feature now upgrades in correct order for APCs using machine guns as primary weapon
 - Raised Generation 3 helicopter rocket pod soft attack above Generation 2 (Issue #5297)
 - Restored missing party popularity gains and penalties in national focuses and events (Issue #5267)
 - Fixed the Currency Strength Target modifier doing nothing, added Minimum Currency Strength and Inflation Gain modifiers, and made silver and bimetallic standards pull harder toward par
 - Fixed the game crashing on macOS when selecting an air zone during an air battle (Issue #540)
 - [ENG] Fixed Close Proximity with NATO overlapping Reservist Corps Act after The Queen's Speech, Social Housing and Fostering Home Ownership reappearing after Thriving Rural Scene, and Thales and BAE Systems being ignored as Watchkeeper WK450 and Nimrod MRA4 designers (Issue #5042)
 - [ENG] Replaced DLC tech prerequisite for moonbase focus with non-DLC tech
 - [IRQ] Fixed the American victory in the Iraq War not registering when the coalition annexes Iraq instead of making it a subject, which kept the Islamic State from ever forming (Issue #5343)
 - [USA] Fixed The Society Collectivizes Victory handing power to the Communist State party, which locked the anarchist path out of its own next focus (Issue #5116)

Database:
 - Moved all per-building, resource, and healthcare GDP factors into named constants (Issue #5301)
 - [POR] Microchip plant (Vila do Conde) and related technologies added
 - [POR] Serviço de Informações Estratégicas de Defesa e Militares (SIEDM) added

OOBs:
 - Fixed most countries Airborne unit templates incapable of doing parachute drops

User Interface:
 - Added decision category unlock tooltips to 44 focuses that open new decisions mid-game (Issue #5257)
 - Added decision category unlock tooltips to 19 focuses, events and decisions that open new decisions mid-game (Issue #5257)
 - Added decision category unlock tooltips to 21 event options that open new decisions mid-game (Issue #5257)

For more details checkout the website at:
https://millenniumdawn.github.io/Millennium-Dawn/changelogs/v2-0-changes-hotfixes/
