AI:
 - An AI in a peacetime deficit now trims a civil spending law each month and lowers what its people expect with it, instead of waiting for a deep crisis, and restores spending once its surplus returns
 - The AI and player corporate tax automation no longer cut corporate tax toward 15% just for having fewer than 10 free civilian factories, which drove budgets into deficit; corporate tax now falls only from a surplus with low debt
 - The AI now stops buying fuel once its storage is 90% full; it used to stop only at a fixed 1 million fuel, which most countries cannot store, so it kept paying for fuel that was thrown away
 - The AI now accepts the renewable energy investment from the Greens and farmers only when its treasury covers the $8.50 Billion cost; it used to borrow the cost, which pushed microstates into a debt default
 - [SOV] Tweaked SOV vehicle design targets to align with recent changes and prioritization weight

Balance:
 - Removed gunship weapons and stealh bomb bays as usable modules on AWACS aircraft; removed stealth bomb bays as usable modules on transport aircraft
