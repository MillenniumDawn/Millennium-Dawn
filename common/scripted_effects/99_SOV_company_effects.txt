# SOV_economy_modifier. Companies the sphere subjects inherit also write SOV_econsub_*,

# are the subject's copy, not a bonus Russia itself receives.
# The six market-driven firms only get their base rung here -- everything above it comes
# from SOV_recalculate_capitalisation_modifier.
