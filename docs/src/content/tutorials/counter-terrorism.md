Millennium Dawn tracks terrorist organizations as their own actors. Each one has a headquarters and a threat level, and each country keeps its own intelligence on them. Manage your external and domestic operations from the **Counter-Terror** screen, which lists terror threat by nation.

## Raids

Counter-terror raids strike an organization directly:

- **High-Value Target Raid** kills or captures members of the organization.
- **Weapons Cache Raid** destroys a weapons cache.
- **Training Camp Raid** destroys a training site.
- **Drone Ops** flies recon and strike missions.
- **Elimination Raid** goes after the headquarters. A successful one removes the organization.

## Other Basics

- Infiltrating an organization lasts until that organization is destroyed.
- Terrorism can escalate internationally. Every country then gets its own event, with effects on stability, the policing budget, and party popularity.
