v2.1.0

Content:
 - Standardized and historical party names for the countries: HEZ, HKG, HOL, IND, ITA, JAP
 - Removed the generic MIO trees; every MIO now uses a unique tree (ALG, ARG, AUS, AZE, BLR, BRM, BUL, CAN, CHL, COL, CZE, DEN, EGY, FIN, GEO, IND, ISR, KOR, MEX, NIG, NKO, NRY, PAK, PER, PHI, POR, RAJ, SAF, SAU, SER, SIN, SPR, SUD, SYR, TUR, UAE, UKR, VEN)
 - Country AI path game rules standardised to Historical / alternate paths / Random Path / No Path: KOS, LIC, NKR, SOO, SMA, TAJ
 - All artillery revised, now they should be more useful from the start
 - [POR] Starting divisions (deployed and templates) revamped
 - [UKR] Added Decision + Events chains to continue work on An-71 AWACS plane (removed from starting equipment)
 - [UKR] Reworked the BRDM-2 modernization decisions and events around a chosen manufacturer
 - [UKR] Added a light utility vehicle tender decision offering the LuAZ-1901 Heoloh, D-041 Step or Dozor-A

AI:
 - An AI in a peacetime deficit now trims a civil spending law each month and lowers what its people expect with it, instead of waiting for a deep crisis, and restores spending once its surplus returns
 - The AI and player corporate tax automation no longer cut corporate tax toward 15% just for having fewer than 10 free civilian factories, which drove budgets into deficit; corporate tax now falls only from a surplus with low debt
 - The AI now stops buying fuel once its storage is 90% full; it used to stop only at a fixed 1 million fuel, which most countries cannot store, so it kept paying for fuel that was thrown away
 - The AI now accepts the renewable energy investment from the Greens and farmers only when its treasury covers the $8.50 Billion cost; it used to borrow the cost, which pushed microstates into a debt default
 - [SOV] Tweaked SOV vehicle design targets to align with recent changes and prioritization weight

Balance:
 - Debt default payments no longer overpay or re-trigger a default: Pay $10 and $50 Billion from Our Treasury pay at most what is owed, can be taken once a week, and Pay $10 borrows at most half of GDP, ending the repeat-default loop small AI countries fell into
 - Allowed central bank policy rates down to -10% to counter deflation (Issue #3974)
 - Added currency floors and inflation relief for players on easier difficulties and AI on harder difficulties. (Issue #5469)
 - Removed gunship weapons and stealh bomb bays as usable modules on AWACS aircraft; removed stealth bomb bays as usable modules on transport aircraft
 - Reduced the starting inflation to 12.6% and increased the starting policy rate of Iran to 8
 - Merged carrier and non-carrier light and medium aircraft MIO groups, so every MIO that builds light or medium aircraft also builds their carrier variants (Issue #5361)
 - Changed wartime equipment upkeep to a flat 1.75x, from 2.25x for players and 1.45x for AI
 - [POL] Shortened 21 of the economic tree's 70-day focuses to match their rewards: 11 small ones now take 35 days and 10 with larger rewards take 49, while the 11 that spend heavily, build or restructure the state keep 70
 - [UKR] Rescheduled the National Bank of Ukraine rate cut events for the 30% starting policy rate, so the rate follows the historical path (29% in April 2000, 27% in August) instead of dropping to 12% within the first year
