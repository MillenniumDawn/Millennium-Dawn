# Company Policy and Political Rewards

Company milestones now ask for federal policy responses. Private acquisitions and sales no longer transfer their transaction prices to the Treasury. Existing public procurement and federal investment remain public expenditures.

## Reward budgets

Ordinary company research and industrial programs provide a one-percent modifier for 180 days. Shared program spirits refresh instead of stacking. Structural milestones retain smaller permanent effects, capped at 0.5 percentage points per contribution.

The permanent budget counts every beneficial source contribution, including mutually exclusive alternatives. This conservative upper bound is at least as large as any reachable route. Contributions to the same modifier are scaled together, with rounding toward zero, when that bound exceeds ten percentage points.

| Modifier                    |  Conservative permanent bound |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------: |
| Factory output              |          10 percentage points |
| Research speed              |          10 percentage points |
| Productivity growth         |        9.75 percentage points |
| Other individual modifiers  | At most 2.5 percentage points |
| Permanent flat productivity |                          None |

[The calculation receipt](companies.json) lists each modifier budget, proportional scaling factor, shared permanent payload, and removed private cash transfer. These are source bounds, not measured GDP or employment effects.

## Public policy and political support

- Ordinary enforcement costs 25 political power. A private financing choice does not charge public funds. Existing identified public contracts retain their amounts.
- Competition-policy responses divide one percentage point of support between progressives and Greens. A less interventionist response gives one point to Libertarians.
- Worker-relief responses divide one point between progressives and democratic socialists. Major public-unease resolutions award two points in total.
- Economic warnings and disruptions offer a funded relief response or private adjustment with a stability cost. These choices retain the existing outcome flags and unlocks.
- Each popularity call sets its beneficiary and outlook increment, then resets the optional outlook parameter. Court cases record resolution when delivered, and company events check their existing mutually exclusive outcomes.

The opening Visio, AOL, Microsoft antitrust, Datapoint, and Mac OS X events use the same policy and support budgets. Private mergers and the Microsoft settlement no longer grant free factories or a national economic-cycle upgrade. A recorded Microsoft review prevents repeat rewards, and an early conduct settlement prevents a second verdict in the later remedy event.

## Corporate History modes

Full mode presents the authored choices. Outcomes Only retains the historical permanent payload through the same guarded effects and omits one-time rewards. Off schedules no optional corporate milestones. The crisis lifecycle is handled separately by the next PR.

## Verification limits

Changed-file hooks and source review cover this change. Content checks run in hosted CI. In-game loading, AI choices, event rendering, and the practical strength of third-party support have not been tested.
