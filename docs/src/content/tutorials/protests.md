The Protests System tracks unrest in your country as **Protest Strength**. Open it with the **Protest Menu** button. The window lists what is pushing protests up this month, so read it before you spend political power.

## What Drives Protests

- **Spending gaps.** Citizens expect a baseline of welfare, health, education, police, administrative, and military spending. Underspending raises Protest Strength. Running out of fuel adds to it.
- **Low stability with elections.** Stability below 20% feeds protests while your government still holds elections.
- **A high policy rate.** A policy rate above 8.9% adds pressure every month.
- **High interest on debt.** An interest rate above 8.9% does the same. With no debt this driver drops out.
- **Recession or depression.** Either adds a random monthly hit. Depression is the worse of the two.

## What You Can Do

- Raise the spending laws the window marks in red.
- Use **Appease Protesters** and **Preemptive Reform** to buy time.
- Make a promise, such as **Promise: Tax Relief for Citizens** or **Promise: Jobs Program**, and then meet its target.
- Fix the cause. Lower the policy rate, pay down debt, or bring the economy out of recession.
