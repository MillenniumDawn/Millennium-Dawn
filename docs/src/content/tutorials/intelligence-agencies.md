Intelligence agencies, operatives, and operations come from the La Résistance expansion and work as they do in the base game. Millennium Dawn adds its own agency upgrades and operations, and a queue that upgrades your agency for you.

## Automated Upgrades

The agency window has an **Automated Upgrades** control with three modes:

- **Upgrades Off**: nothing is upgraded automatically.
- **Upgrades On**: queued upgrades go first. If none can start, the game picks a valid upgrade for you.
- **Queue Only**: only the upgrades you queued are started.

Add upgrades to the **Queue**, then raise or lower their priority. Shift-click moves an upgrade to the top or bottom. **Clear Queue** empties it.

You cannot upgrade the agency by hand while an automated upgrade is in progress. Cancelling that upgrade does not stop the next one. Choose **Upgrades Off** to stop further automated upgrades. Clearing the queue stops further upgrades only in **Queue Only** mode; **Upgrades On** can still pick a valid upgrade.
