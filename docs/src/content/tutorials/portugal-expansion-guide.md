title: "Portugal: An Expansionist Run"
description: "What a maximalist Portuguese campaign looks like, from an indebted EU member in 2000 to a crown over Brazil, Iberia, the old Estado da Índia and the western Mediterranean."
---

Portugal starts in 2000 small, in debt and tied into the European Union and NATO. A maximalist
run turns it into a crown that rules Brazil and the River Plate, unites Iberia, takes back Goa,
Malacca and Macau, and holds the old Mediterranean islands of the Crown of Aragon. It is a chain of
short wars and special mechanics, and you pay for all of it while the economy grows underneath.

This guide shows one way the run can go. For what each focus does, see the
[Portugal page](/countries/portugal/).

## The Shape of the Run

1. **Build the instrument** (2000s): the economy, the armed forces and the shadow war.
2. **Restore the crown** (2000s to 2010s): ideology, the Crown and a referendum.
3. **Unite Iberia** (2010s to 2030s): a war on Spain or a union with it.
4. **Take Brazil** (2010s to 2030s): a puppet war, then the Southern Crown in South America.
5. **Return to the East** (2020s to 2040s): Macau, the Estado da Índia and a war with China.
6. **Close the Mediterranean** (late game): the Crown of Aragon and a League of Crowns.

The dates are rough. Stages overlap, and most players run two or three at once.

## Three Rules for the Whole Run

- **Borrow.** No focus waits for money in the treasury. If a focus costs more than you hold, the
  rest is borrowed. Watch the interest on your debt, not your cash.
- **Stay in NATO while you fight its members.** NATO only defends a member from an attacker
  outside the alliance. While Portugal is a member, a war on Spain or Italy stays a war with
  Spain or Italy. Leave NATO and the same war brings in the whole alliance.
- **Watch exposure and Brussels.** Hostile focuses and shadow-war decisions raise exposure.
  Wars on EU members also count against Portugal in the EU's own breach rules. At high exposure
  Brussels gives you a year to stand down or leave the Union.

## 1. Build the Instrument

Start with the economy. The Programme of Government sets your policies, and Regional
Development builds factories, infrastructure and research facilities region by region. Its later
projects open by date and build more for the time they take, so it lasts the whole game.

At the same time, take the Lusophone Community and Lusophone Realpolitik. This is the shadow
war: build an intelligence network in a former colony, infiltrate its government and army, buy
influence, start a rising, send volunteers to the side you created, and puppet whoever wins.
The Campaigns are short focuses, mostly 21 days, that you can slot between economic ones. Each ends in a
friendly or hostile road. The hostile road opens that country to the shadow war.

The armed forces trees build research facilities and ahead-of-time research programs. You do
not need all of them. Pick the service you will fight with: the army for Spain and Brazil, the
navy for everything overseas.

Come back to the services as the run goes on. New focuses open when you research a new
generation of equipment, when your fleet, army or air force grows, and when you gain a subject
or territory in a new region. A puppet in Africa, South America or Asia unlocks training and
bases there that make your forces better at fighting in that region.

## 2. Restore the Crown

Most of the expansion needs a king. Work down the Ideological Question. Every change of
government and every policy raises ideological development. The Crown sits at the end of the
Battle of Ideas, after the Greatest Portuguese.

The Crown needs the CDS-PP, Nós or the monarchists (PPM) in government and development above
60. Each branch of the Ideological Question ends in its own regime, and only one can be taken. The monarchy referendum needs stability above 45% to call. It passes if the monarchists
hold more than 20% or stability is above 60%. If it fails, the focus resets and you can try
again.

Without a king you can still take Brazil through Terra de Vera Cruz, but the Southern Crown and
the League of Crowns are closed to you.

## 3. Unite Iberia

The Balance of Powers column holds the big neighbors. For Spain, take the Almaraz Complaint.
After 2012, Aljubarrota gives a war goal on Galicia, Ceuta and Spain's islands. The Olivenza
Incident instead turns a recent false flag into a war goal on all of Spain. Either war needs a
government that is not of Spain's own ideology, or a nationalist, fascist or non-aligned one.
While you fight, the Spanish Spoils decisions take what you occupy.

The peaceful road is A Union of the Peninsula after 2025, which asks Madrid to join.

Once you hold the peninsula, through the union or by conquering Spain, the Dream of Iberia
tree opens. Keep every region's
sectarianism down until you can proclaim the Iberian Union. Then the Gates of Iberia ask
London for Gibraltar, Morocco for Tangier and Andorra to join.

## 4. Take Brazil

The Brazil branch opens with the Treaty of Porto Seguro, then a friendly road or the hostile
Contest for Luanda. Both lead to the Treaty of Rio. With monarchists in power, the Braganza
Restoration gives a puppet war goal on Brazil. A nationalist, fascist or military government
takes Terra de Vera Cruz instead, claims Bahia and Pernambuco, and then the Sunrise Invasion
puppets the rest.

With a king and Brazil as your subject, proclaim the United Kingdom of Portugal, Brazil and the
Algarves. It annexes Brazil and cores its states. From there:

- move the court to Rio de Janeiro;
- take Uruguay and then puppet Argentina, puppet Paraguay, and take Bolivia's Pando-Beni;
- take French Guiana in a border war, which does not call in France's allies;
- offer Panama a treaty for the canal, or take it if Panama refuses.

The South American Crown cores the conquests.

## 5. Return to the East

The China branch starts with Forum Macao. When Washington puts China under a trade war or an
embargo, the Macau Free Port focus offers to take Macau back as a free port for 60 billion and a
monthly rent. China has to agree. If no trade war comes, this road stays shut. Full Sovereignty annexes the city, and China
contests it in a border war.

With Macau held and a government that is not democratic, the Estado da Índia opens:

- Goa, Damão and Diu from India, then Malacca, then Singapore as a puppet;
- Solor and Flores from Indonesia once you hold East Timor, then the Spice Islands;
- Hong Kong, which China defends.

The Macau Window decisions sell visas, passports and a tax haven to Chinese officials and money.
Each one weakens China and adds a grievance. Every third grievance, China retaliates with six
months of trade pressure. With three grievances, A Second Opium War claims Guangdong, Fujian,
Zhejiang and Shanghai. China is a great power, so build the navy and the army before you start.

## 6. Close the Mediterranean

After any Gates of Iberia focus, the Crown of Aragon claims the old Aragonese Mediterranean:

- ask Italy to free Sardinia, then protect it or take it;
- take the Roussillon from France in a border war;
- claim Naples and Sicily, then puppet Malta.

Italy is in NATO. Fight it while Portugal is still a member and the war stays with Italy.

Last, the League of Crowns. Fund royalist movements across Europe, back a restoration when a
country is weak, and invite the new monarchies into a defensive league. Founding it means
leaving NATO, so do it after your wars on NATO members are over. A country led by a player is
only ever offered help, so you cannot overthrow a friend's republic.

## Ending the Wars

You rarely need to grind an enemy to capitulation. Once you fully hold every state you claim
from an enemy, or hold its capital while it is close to surrender, you can offer peace: those
states for the war's end, or protection as your puppet. It can refuse, so keep fighting while
you wait.

When an enemy does capitulate, a treaty event lets you draw the border yourself, the day after
any peace conference. Spain can be held to its claims, broken into its nations or annexed. Italy
can lose the Aragonese south and Sardinia. Britain can lose Gibraltar and the islands Portuguese
captains found, or the Union itself. With a king on the throne, you can also put crowns on
Rome, Paris, London and Rio as your puppets.

## Where It Goes Wrong

- **Debt.** Every stage costs money. If interest outruns growth, pause the wars and build.
- **Too many fronts.** Brazil, India and China are all an ocean away. Finish one war before the
  next, and keep naval supply in mind.
- **Leaving NATO too early.** Any later war on a member becomes a war with the whole alliance.
- **Losing the crown.** Elections continue under a restored monarchy. If the monarchists lose
  power, focuses that need them in office, such as the Braganza Restoration, wait until they
  are back.
