Portugal starts in 2000 with a socialist government, membership of the European Union, the
Eurozone and NATO, five divisions of which two garrison islands, and a debt eight times the
size of its treasury. It also starts with more influence in six former colonies than any
other power holds there, and no instrument between a diplomatic note and a war.

The tree is about building that instrument.

## Political Situation

The Socialist Party governs with 45.2% support. The Social Democrats are the alternative
government at 33.1%, the CDU holds 9.2% on the left and the CDS-PP 8.5% on the right. The
Braganças and the national right are rounding errors that some branches of the tree do not
leave as rounding errors.

## The Tree

Five columns, each with its own button at the top of the tree: Ideology (the Ideological
Question), Economy (the Programme of Government over Regional Development), Armed Forces (the
Portuguese Sea and the Armed Forces over the Air Force), Lusophone World (the Lusophone Community
over the Campaigns) and The Powers (the Balance of Powers over the Dream of Iberia). The European Union's shared
trees sit below the first two columns.

- **Lusophone Community**: the Camões Institute and economic diplomacy, the civilian and
  officer networks and the Legion, the intelligence service, the European question and the
  Fifth Empire.
- **Ideological Question**: an ideological development meter that rises on every change of
  government and every policy enacted, and gates the radical content behind it. Four branches:
  - **The Battle of Ideas**: the party press and the institutes, then the Casino Conferences
    and a choice of tradition. Down one side are the Hidden King, Integralismo Lusitano,
    Pessoa's Mensagem and Lusotropicalism. Down the other are Seara Nova, Agostinho da Silva,
    Eduardo Lourenço and the critics of Lusotropicalism. Both meet at the Greatest Portuguese,
    where the country crowns Salazar, Cunhal or Aristides de Sousa Mendes.
  - **Public Opinion**: focuses that move popularity and put people in the street, then a
    general strike or a state of exception. Either one opens the Desperate Generation and
    Revolutionary Rupture, a revolution the AI never takes. The Desperate Generation leads on to
    the Troika protests, a presidential dissolution that calls an early election, and the new
    parties of the right.
  - **The Memory of April**: Grândola, the captains, the first May Day and the Constituent
    Assembly, then the Hot Summer or the 25th of November and the Constitution of 1976. The four
    founders follow: Cunhal, Soares, Sá Carneiro and Freitas do Amaral. The left's Contraption
    or the right's Democratic Alliance follows, depending on who won the last election.
  - **International Solidarity**: alignment focuses that open Beijing, Washington, the
    sovereigntist bloc, BRICS or the Lusophone world depending on who is in office, then Europe
    with Us or Proudly Alone.
- **Armed Forces**, **Portuguese Sea** and **Air Force**: professionalization,
  procurement, and a doctrine school in each service. Almost nothing is mutually exclusive;
  the time and the money make the choice. Each service can buy a research facility for
  15 billion early on: Santa Margarida and the Viana do Castelo yards open right after the
  service's first focus, and the Évora aeronautics park after the F-16 upgrade. Behind each
  facility are five research programs for 25 billion each that cut three years off the penalty
  for researching ahead of time. None of them is needed; they are for a player with money to
  spare. A program finished before the older technologies in its line are researched spends
  its uses on those instead.
- **Programme of Government**: the policy tree: financial, industrial and social policy, each
  a set of two-sided forks. Taking a side commits the country to it, and reversing it needs
  the other bloc to win a majority first. The reversal undoes the focuses as well as the
  effects, so an optimal set of policies means flipping governments deliberately. The
  branch also carries the court of auditors, the coalition agreements, and most of the
  reforms that improve the four starting spirits; the rest are in regional development.
  The wealth tax raises productivity growth and the flat tax lowers it. Retail CBDC
  (40 billion) gives every citizen an account at the central bank: inside the euro it is the
  digital euro, with the escudo back it is Portugal's own. The Sovereign Wealth Fund puts
  100 billion into international investments. It trades 6% of tax revenue and 10% of
  political power gain for 3% more return on investment.
- **Regional Development**: one focus per region, each building in the state it names, plus
  the Lisbon-Porto high-speed line, the southern freight corridor, Leixões, the Douro,
  Autoeuropa, Sines, Alqueva, the Algarve's water and the islands' air links. Each opens only
  when a matching policy focus is done, and the cohesion funds at the end are
  free while Portugal is in the Union. Infrastructure, network infrastructure and rail
  terminals also need the railway or internet technology for the next level; where it is
  missing, the focus gives research toward it instead, at no cost.
- **Campaigns**: one chain per former colony, built on what actually happened there.
- **Balance of Powers**: the Campaigns for powers that can answer back: Spain, the United
  Kingdom, the United States, China, Brazil and, if it forms without Portugal, the European
  Federation, each with a friendly and a hostile road.

Policy and industry focuses also move the interest groups: the wealth tax angers the
landowners, a flat tax pleases business and angers the unions, and Chinese contractors and
planners anger the unions and the landowners.

## The Shadow War

The loop is: build an intelligence network in a former colony, infiltrate its government and
army, buy influence there, sponsor a rising, send volunteers to the side you created, and puppet
whoever wins. Everything runs on three things the game already tracks: intelligence network
strength (built by operatives), the civilian and army infiltration assets (from the infiltration
operations and the Buy the Officer Corps decision), and influence (from the Cultivate Influence
decision and the Campaign focuses). Portugal can send volunteers at any level of world tension.

Most of the shadow war's decisions sit in the Lusophone Realpolitik category, which is open from
the start. Its description is the tutorial, and its header shows the exposure meter in large type
above a grid of flags in three sections. The Lusosphere is Angola, UNITA, Mozambique, Guinea-Bissau, Cape
Verde, São Tomé, East Timor, Cabinda, Equatorial Guinea and Brazil. Each opens when Portugal takes
its hostile focus: the one at the end of its Campaign, or the Contest for Luanda for Brazil. The
Iberian section is Spain, which opens with the Almaraz Complaint, and Andorra and Morocco. They and
the Hispanosphere (Spanish-speaking America, the Philippines and Western Sahara) open when
Portugal proclaims the Iberian Union. A Directed Rising in Brazil or Spain is how Portugal gets
the civil war that the Aid Landing Ground and Protect the Portuguese Galicians need. Gibraltar has
no flag because it is a British state, not a country.
Click a flag to see only that country's decisions, and click it again (or Show All) to see them
all. The game starts with Angola selected. The filter is only for the player: an AI Portugal sees
every country's decisions. A flag that cannot be selected is grayed out, and its tooltip lists
the conditions. Most decisions in the category take 30 to 60 days: the cost is paid when one
starts and the result lands when it ends. The Madrid Operation takes 180 days, and the Brussels
Ultimatum is a 365-day mission (see below).

Four other categories hold the rest. The Overseas War School, opened by the African War School,
sends an officer on a 120-day tour of a war Portugal has a hand in. The Imperial Conference,
opened by its focus, asks each Lusophone subject to join a federation. The Spanish Spoils are
described below. Counter-Intelligence belongs to the other side: a country carrying the second
or third tier of the Portuguese Presence spirit can spend 90 days purging Portugal's network
there.

Backing a side in a war that was already running, such as Angola's, pays out when that side wins:
at least 25% influence there (more for an underdog), better trade relations, an infiltrated
government, political power and the resource rights of one of its states. A war Portugal started
through the Directed Rising pays more: the winner becomes a subject, and Portugal gains 50%
influence there, trade relations, the resource rights of one of its states, 100 political power,
stability and war support.

A country that has just come out of a civil war is weak. Destabilise (unlocked by War by Proxy)
costs it 8% stability; within three years of the war, once it is below 30% stability and Portugal
holds over 50% influence and a 70% network there, the Protectorate decision (unlocked by the
Protectorate focus) gives a war goal to invade it and keep it as a puppet.

Backing the weaker side, or a side the world has outlawed, pays more influence and more reward.
Only an outlawed side (a rebel movement, an unrecognized government or a sanctioned country)
also raises the exposure meter faster. Exposure is what Brussels sees. Past a threshold it counts
against Portugal in the European Union's own breach machinery. At tier three, if Portugal is
still a member, Brussels issues the Brussels Ultimatum: a 365-day mission to choose between
standing down (Remain in Europe) and leaving (Atlantic Exit). Letting it run out costs
stability, political power and more exposure.

Overseas intervention costs money as well as logistics. The Portuguese Legion is a weekly expense
of 0.2 billion for each country Portuguese volunteers are in. On top of that, fuel consumption,
equipment attrition and supply consumption rise with the number of those countries, which falls
hardest on formations Portugal cannot replace.

**The Lusophone Community** builds the instruments: a foreign intelligence service, an
operations section, the civilian and officer networks, and the Special Actions Detachment.

**Neo-colonialism** buys the Mining Concession, the Defence Agreement and the Economic
Protectorate. The Defence Agreement takes military access only from Portugal's client
governments, the rebel governments its risings set up. The directed rising is an operation, not a
decision. The focus that unlocks it needs an intelligence agency with at least one upgrade and two
operatives. The operation itself needs 30% influence, 70% intelligence network strength, an
infiltrated government and army, a recent false-flag raid there, and 5,000 small arms of any
model. It starts the civil war with Portuguese politics in the rebel government. The False Flag
itself does only one thing: it costs the target 5% stability and leaves its regime looking for
someone to blame, which is the pretext the rising needs for 180 days.

**The campaigns** are the list of places to use the instruments, eight chains. Each one opens
with a diplomatic focus that gives influence and opinion, runs through one cooperation focus
(Angola, Guinea-Bissau, São Tomé and Cape Verde) or two (the rest) that add influence and
modifiers, and ends in a choice between a friendly road and a hostile one. None of them grant infiltration; that is done by hand through the operations. Angola is the war that is already running on day one and Portugal observes it
from Estoril with a foot in both camps. Guinea-Bissau is the coup state, where every officer
above major did his staff course in Lisbon and two tons of cocaine a week come ashore in
the Bijagós. Mozambique is the dam Portugal still owned in 2000, the training mission and the
Rovuma gas. East Timor only exists from 2002. Equatorial Guinea joins the club in 2014 and
brings the Black Beach file with it. Cabinda is not a country yet. Cape Verde is the one
where nobody gets shot, and São Tomé is one island at the point every West African tanker
has to pass.

The two roads are mutually exclusive. The friendly focus is a treaty, a patronage or a
settlement. For Guinea-Bissau, Mozambique, East Timor, Equatorial Guinea and the two sides of
Angola it is an offer the other side can refuse; a refusal uncompletes the focus, so the other
road opens, and that country will not hear the offer again for a year. The hostile focus
usually asks nobody. It costs exposure and a clear opinion penalty with the target, and it opens
the Lusophone Realpolitik decisions and operations against that country. Angola is the
exception, because the hostile road against one side is the friendly road to the other. Support
UNITA (against Angola) and Recognise Luanda (against UNITA) are offers the backed side can refuse.
Only if it accepts do they cost exposure and open the decisions against the other side. The
other hostile roads are the
Night of the Generals, the Return of RENAMO, the Petitioners, Severo Moto's Hour, the Malongo
Fields against Cabinda, the Question of Praia and the Sixteenth of July.

**The powers** are the Campaigns for countries that can answer back. Every Portugal can take
them. Each power has a friendly and a hostile road. Two of them are still open to the shadow war:
the hostile Contest for Luanda opens Brazil to the Lusophone Realpolitik decisions, and the Almaraz
Complaint opens Spain. Spain: the Iberian summits, the
electricity market and the Albufeira water convention, or de-Spanishing, the Almaraz Complaint
and Aljubarrota. The Almaraz Complaint also opens the False Flag operation
in Spain, and the Olivenza Incident turns a recent false flag into a war goal on
all of Spain; Aljubarrota and the Olivenza Incident exclude each other. Either road ends, after 2025, in a proposal of union
(see below). The United Kingdom: the Falklands and Gibraltar declarations, the Hereford
courses, the Royal Navy's pre-1985 ships and a carrier built at Rosyth, delivered two years
after London takes the order, or the memory of the
1890 Ultimatum. Royal Navy Licences costs a flat 8 billion, paid to London for the British
shipbuilding experts it sends. It grants naval research bonuses and licenses for the newest
British frigates, mine-hunter and landing ship, plus the newest destroyer and patrol boat if
London has built a modern one. The United States: Lajes, the Azores summit, an F-16 upgrade and the Atlantic Centre, or
ending the base agreement and leaving NATO. China: Macau, the Crisis Privatisations, golden
visas and the Sines terminal, or screening Chinese capital. Forum Macao also opens Shenzhen
supply chains (industry and production research), Chinese contractors and Chinese planners
(construction speed). Brazil: the Treaty of Porto Seguro, then the Statute of Equality, a
stake in the pre-salt fields, Embraer at Évora (which Brazil can refuse) and championing
EU-Mercosur, or the Contest for Luanda, rejecting the spelling agreement and the Oi veto, then the
Treaty of Rio. From there a monarchist government can take a puppet war goal on Brazil and a
nationalist, fascist or military government can claim Bahia and Pernambuco; democratic and communist
governments cannot declare war on a government of their own ideology.
The Macanese passports bring two years of arrivals from Macau: more migration, faster research
and faster productivity growth. A focus that deals with one country improves that country's
trade relations with Portugal; Gibraltar with Madrid, for example, gives +20 with Spain.

**Macau.** While Washington has China under a trade war or an embargo, Portugal can offer to
take Macau back as a free port that carries Chinese goods past the sanctions. China answers
the offer. If it accepts, Portugal pays 60 billion up front, booked as 60 billion of
international investment, and 1 billion a month in rent. Macau becomes a Portuguese puppet,
and China takes the Macau Free Port spirit, which makes Chinese resources 15% cheaper for
Portugal: each civilian factory Portugal spends on Chinese trade buys about one more unit of
every resource. Full Sovereignty annexes Macau outright. That ends the rent and the trade
discount, and China keeps up to 60 billion of Portugal's investments. Two Macau Garrison
brigades are raised in the city, and the People's Liberation Army contests it in a border war
from the Pearl River Delta. If Portugal wins, Macau stays Portuguese; if it loses, the city
returns to China; if neither side breaks within 90 days, the fighting stops and Macau stays
Portuguese. However it ends, Beijing then answers: while Portugal holds the city, China can
impose sanctions, take a war goal on Macau or protest; if China has retaken it, China can
impose sanctions or welcome Macau home.

**The Gates of Iberia** open once the union is proclaimed. Portugal can ask the United Kingdom for
Gibraltar, which London is likely to grant if Portugal kept the oldest alliance, in return for a
British base and military access; ask Morocco for Tanger-Tetouan-Al Hoceima, which Rabat will refuse,
giving Portugal a war goal; and ask Andorra to join. A week after taking the Moroccan region,
Portugal can end that war with a white peace that keeps it, or refuse and fight on against a Morocco
defending its own cores harder. The union's formal name follows the government (the Iberian Union,
Socialist Union, Confederation, State or Emirate), and so does the proclamation focus.

The European Federation, if it forms without Portugal: bilateral agreements and an associated
partnership, or an Atlantic identity and closing the exclusive economic zone. What Portugal
asks of London, Washington, Beijing and Madrid arrives as an event they answer, and a refusal
uncompletes the focus so it can be asked again later.

**The open hand** drops the pretense. Three countries have a province Portugal can take while
they fight a civil war, each a 30-day mission. Establish an Aid Landing Ground takes Espírito
Santo and expands its airfield, because Brazil is an ocean away and helping anyone there needs a
local base. Protect the Portuguese Galicians uses the cultural and ethnic ties as a pretext to
take Galicia. Secure Cahora Bassa takes Tete and the dam Portugal built. When the war ends,
Portugal decides whether to hand the province back only if the winner is a government its own
rising installed: it can return it for influence or keep it. Any other winner, including a side
Portugal only backed, demands it back, and keeping it then gives that country a claim and a war
goal. If the side that
owned the province is annexed, the claim passes to the winner. At the far end there is a file on
Madrid, and after 2012 the Aljubarrota focus writes down claims on Galicia, Ceuta and Spain's
island states with a war goal to match.

**The Spanish Spoils** open whenever Portugal is at war with Spain, or Spain is fighting a
civil war Portugal started or took a side in. Four decisions take Galicia, Ceuta, the Canary
Islands and the Balearic Islands: whatever Portugal occupies, or, except for Galicia, whatever
a Spanish side at war with itself holds and is not fighting Portugal over. Every one raises
exposure.

## The Iberian Union

The Iberian Union is no longer a formable decision. Spain and Portugal form it through a small
shared focus tree, The Dream of Iberia, which opens once the country owns and fully controls
mainland Spain, the Balearic Islands and mainland Portugal (Gibraltar and Andorra are not
needed). Portugal gets there through A Union of the Peninsula, which asks Madrid to join, or
by already holding the peninsula.

Every region has a sectarianism score from 0 to 100, shown on a map in the Dream of an
Iberian Union decisions. Select a region on the map, then act on it:

- **Integration** (Iberian Civic Service, Centralised Schooling) raises its sectarianism now
  but lowers what it gains each week.
- **Concessions** (Regional Subsidies, Autonomy Statute) lower it now but raise what it
  gains each week.
- **Language classes** lower the union's sectarian growth, which starts at +0.25% a week, for
  an ongoing cost. Below 40% stability the union's growth cannot go below 0%.

The first focus already proclaims the country the Iberian Union and cores the union's
states; the rest of the tree is about holding it together. Sectarianism has two parts, the
score and its weekly growth, and each focus and decision shows the part it changes. The
tree builds a common curriculum, broadcasting, a single market and common citizenship, and
makes the capital choice: Lisbon or Madrid. Each capital calms its own side, angers the other
and gains 5 victory points. The Madrid-Lisbon High-Speed Line is optional. It opens once the
railway along the route is at level 6, and it calms the states it runs through and lowers the
union's sectarian growth. Once every region reaches 0, the union can be proclaimed: that ends
sectarianism and gives two years of National Unity (+10% stability, war support and political
power). A region that reaches 100 declares independence if it has a nation to break away as, and
the government either lets it go or goes to war to keep it. A region with no such nation falls
into unrest instead: the union takes the Regional Unrest spirit until its sectarianism drops
below 100.

## Military Leadership

Portugal starts with the officers who held command on 1 January 2000: General Martins
Barrento at the head of the Army, General Espírito Santo as chief of the general staff,
Admiral Vieira Matias at the head of the Navy and General Alvarenga at the head of the Air
Force. The five starting brigades and military zones carry their 2000 commanders. Later chiefs
of staff join the pool on the date they took office.

## AI Behavior

The `POR_ai_behavior` game rule offers Historical, Lusophone, Imperial, Random Path and No
Path. Historical Portugal works the economy and the policy tree and leaves the networks
alone. Lusophone runs the cultivation and the puppeting. Imperial takes it to the Fifth
Empire.
