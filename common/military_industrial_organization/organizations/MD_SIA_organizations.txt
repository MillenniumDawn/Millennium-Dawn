## Thailand: Thai Military Industrial Organizations
## Eight MIOs covering the Royal Thai defence industry, each with a custom trait tree.
## All equipment_type / research_categories / equipment_bonus / org-modifier tokens use
## Millennium Dawn's native MIO vocabulary so the companies appear in the correct Defense
## Companies window and their bonuses apply in-game.
##
## Design intent (v0.3): each org is a full branching trait tree in MD's enriched style —
##   * task_capacity + tree_header_text category tabs group the tree into readable columns.
##   * signature capstone traits hit 0.15–0.20 on the stat the real firm is known for.
##   * branching trees offer real mutually_exclusive doctrine choices (saturation vs
##     precision, survivability vs mobility, F-16 vs Gripen, ISR vs strike UAV, fast-attack
##     vs low-observable, licensed vs indigenous rifle).
##   * research/production trade-offs match each firm's identity: R&D houses (DTI, TAI,
##     RV Connex) lead on research/funds; mass-production firms (Chaiseri, Marsun, DID)
##     trade research for cheaper, faster output.
##
## Each org defines an initial_trait (required for custom, non-`include` orgs to load) and
## every trait uses the standard free_trait_picks / small_expenditure completion cost,
## matching MD's other custom-tree countries.
## Art: all using generic GFX slots pending custom icons.

## ── DTI — Defence Technology Institute ─────────────────────────
## Founded 2008; modeled from game start for playability. National R&D agency — the
## research powerhouse of the tree. Real programmes: DTI-1/DTI-2 guided MLRS, the
## D-Eyes UAV family, and a domestic short-range air-defence R&D effort. Four branches:
## guided rocket artillery (guided-MLRS vs short-range ballistic doctrine fork), loitering
## munitions & C4ISR research, domestic small-arms R&D, and short/medium-range air defence
## (SAM vs AA-gun doctrine fork).
