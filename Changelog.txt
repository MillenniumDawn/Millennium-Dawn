It adds no new slots below 50 Political Power and releases every slot only when Political Power goes negative. The AI takes one influence action a month, on each of its targets in turn: military aid first, then puppeting, coups, political manipulation, targeting rival influencers, economic aid for struggling partners, and exploitation of rivals.
