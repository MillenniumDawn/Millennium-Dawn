Hello and welcome to the dev team! Before you can enter the developer channels, please read the following.

## Roles within the dev team

- <span class="dev-team-role dev-team-role--junior">Junior Developer</span>: Newly joined coders who want to help with the mod. You will first be assigned a project and are expected to help to the best of your ability.
- <span class="dev-team-role dev-team-role--developer">Developer</span>: Coders who have contributed substantially to the mod or demonstrated valuable coding skills.
- <span class="dev-team-role dev-team-role--senior">Senior Developer</span>: Contributors who have proven their competence and need little guidance. Their seniority reflects experience; unlike lead developers, they do not make decisions on behalf of the mod.
- <span class="dev-team-role dev-team-role--inactive">Inactive Dev</span>: Coders who reached at least Developer rank and are taking a break from modding. In most cases, please do not bother them.
- <span class="dev-team-role dev-team-role--lead">Lead Developer</span>: Longstanding, highly trusted developers who help decide how the mod develops and proceeds.
- <span class="dev-team-role dev-team-role--council">Council Member</span>: The top level of the team. Council members have the final say on mod-related matters.

## The channels

The general work channels are always relevant to the mod's ongoing development. Other work channels are organized by their expected release, with the next upcoming release listed first.

**Keep team chat, development discussion, and feedback in the appropriate channels rather than in DMs.** This lets the team see what is happening and keeps the project transparent.

Tabled Reworks contains projects that received some development but whose coder stopped working or became unavailable. These projects are not in active development and may never be completed. Archive contains old channels.

## The first thing to do

We are a helpful team. If you have suggestions or need help, ask in the appropriate channel or ping another team member.

First, [set up your GitHub account using this guide](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1V8DLowqEOSmlgazlHeC-hLZzLki5e6cWhQO_ZK6HVYs/edit). Once you have finished, send your GitHub username to **DROID** if you are a <span class="dev-team-role dev-team-role--junior">Junior Developer</span>. If you have questions during setup, please ping a team member. GitHub access is required to contribute to the mod.

If you are a GFX designer, ping or DM **Amtoj** for help getting set up.

## Trial Period

As a <span class="dev-team-role dev-team-role--junior">Junior Developer</span>, you have a one-month trial period. Your GitHub access expires after one month unless it is renewed. We will continue to renew it while you remain active, until you have completed enough work to be promoted to <span class="dev-team-role dev-team-role--developer">Developer</span>.

We understand that everyone has a personal life and that things come up. We still expect you to respond when the team is tagged to check who is available. If you stop responding, you may be removed from the team until you resume communication.

At the end of each month, we review team members' activity to decide whether to renew GitHub access. During the month, we or your mentor may ask for a project status update. If you do not respond, you risk being removed from the team. If your GitHub access expires, you will also be removed from the development team on Discord.

<span class="dev-team-role dev-team-role--junior">Junior Developers</span> cannot start new projects. Because many projects have gone unfinished in the past, you must first complete your assigned work and show that you can contribute reliably. Starting new projects is a responsibility of <span class="dev-team-role dev-team-role--developer">Developers</span>. Once promoted, you can choose projects of your own. This helps us avoid a backlog of unfinished work.
